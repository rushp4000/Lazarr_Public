How Lazarr works

1Grab — instant, nothing downloaded When Sonarr/Radarr grabs a release, Lazarr records it and places a file link in your library. Nothing is added to your TorBox account.
2Play — streamed on demand When you press play (Plex, Jellyfin…), Lazarr adds the torrent to TorBox and streams the bytes straight to your player. First play takes a few extra seconds.
3Clean up — automatic After it sits unwatched for the idle window, Lazarr removes it from TorBox again. Your library file keeps working — the next play just repeats step 2.

Slow lane

For releases TorBox has not cached and that would take longer than a normal wait: old shows, multi-season packs, thin swarms. Lazarr asks TorBox to download them in the background, a few at a time, with no time limit, and checks back periodically. When TorBox has one cached, Lazarr checks its files (no executables, no archives, a real video) and hands it to the arr: it appears in the arr's download client as a completed download, or, for a download the arr already gave up on, Lazarr clears that blocklist entry and asks the arr to search again. Nothing is streamed until the arr imports it.

The slow lane is off. Turn it on under Settings → Slow lane (restart required). Items left from an earlier run are still listed and can be cancelled.
Or drop .torrent files anywhere on this card: they go to the arr selected above, with their trackers. Private-tracker torrents are refused (TorBox would announce with your passkey).

Dangling library links

Library files (symlinks your arrs imported) that point into Lazarr for a release Lazarr no longer has. They fail to play, and the arr does not notice. The list is checked every hour. With repair.unlink_orphans they are removed and the arr rescans, so it marks the files missing and searches again.

TorBox account compliance

Lazarr promises to never hoard content on your TorBox account. The audit compares what TorBox says is on your account with what Lazarr believes it added; a leak means something was left behind and should be investigated.

Runs automatically every 5 minutes.

Torrents on the account that no Lazarr release uses

Listed by every audit: not playing, not a wait download, older than 10 minutes. “In catalog” means a Lazarr release whose TorBox copy was left behind; otherwise something else added it (another app on the same key, a manual add, or a pre-1.2.8 wait-download leak). Lazarr deletes them only if audit.delete_orphans_older_than is set.

Empty folder cleanup

Deleted releases leave empty folders in the download folder, and library files removed by the repair sweeps leave empty season/series/movie folders. Lazarr checks every hour; while removal is off it only logs what it would remove (metric lazarr_empty_dirs). Only folders that contain nothing but other empty folders are removed, one at a time and never recursively; links are never followed. The download category folders, the library root folders and everything above them are never removed, and neither is the folder of any release still in the arr's download list. Restart required.

e.g. 1h, 6h; 5m..168h
